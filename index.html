Why this model

NCCB is a Texas OCCC Chapter 394 licensed Debt Management Organization. That license is an asset when field staff show up as educators and qualifiers — not as closers. The field team's only job at any event is to determine whether a prospect looks like a fit for credit counseling or a debt management plan, and to hand qualified prospects to the licensed sales desk for the actual enrollment conversation and disclosures.

This keeps every enrollment conversation, fee disclosure, and contract signature inside the office, where it's recorded, compliant, and consistent — while still giving the field team full credit for the lead.

Who staffs the field

  • Engagement rep(s): work the table, run the qualifier conversation, capture contact info and consent.
  • Laptop + phone line: live connection to the sales desk during office hours for real-time handoff.
  • Private consult seat: a partitioned, sound-buffered chair where a qualified prospect can take a live transfer call or be heard clearly by the sales rep on video.

What "qualify only" means on the floor

  • Ask a handful of screening questions (debt load, income stability, hardship indicators).
  • Explain what a DMP or counseling engagement generally involves, in plain terms.
  • Capture name, contact info, and contact-consent — nothing more.
  • Hand off live if the sales desk is open; schedule a callback if it is not.

Compliance & Handoff Workflow

The field table is a qualification point, not a point of sale. This protects the prospect, the license, and the close rate — enrollment conversations are more effective in a controlled office setting with a licensed advisor than at a folding table next to a stranger's booth.

1. ApproachRep opens with the qualifier script. No mention of specific rates or savings figures.
→
2. Screen3–5 question qualifier (debt load, income, hardship). Mark Qualified / Maybe / Not a fit.
→
3. ConsentProspect signs/checks contact consent (TCPA) before any callback is scheduled.
→
4. SeatQualified prospect moves to the private consult chair.
→
5. HandoffLive transfer to sales desk (office hours) via laptop/phone, or scheduled callback (after hours).
→
6. LogRep logs the interaction in the field log before moving to the next prospect.
Never at the table: quote specific fees or savings, take a signature on an enrollment agreement, collect a payment or bank/card detail, or promise a specific program outcome.
Always at the table: post the OCCC license number, keep the fee-schedule handout available on request, get contact consent before scheduling any callback, and route every enrollment conversation to a licensed sales rep.

Live transfer vs. scheduled callback

ScenarioAction
Sales desk open, prospect qualifiesLive transfer: rep calls the desk extension from the laptop/phone line, prospect moves to the private consult seat and takes the call or video handoff directly.
Sales desk closed, prospect qualifiesRep books a callback window on the shared calendar (see Weekly Calendar tab) and logs the appointment in the field log; prospect gets a confirmation.
Prospect does not qualifyRep thanks them, offers the general financial-literacy handout only, logs as "Not a fit" — no follow-up contact without separate consent.
Note: contact-consent language should be reviewed against current TCPA and Texas OCCC advertising/solicitation rules before this goes to print — this deck lays out the operational flow, not legal sign-off.

Weekly Outreach Calendar — Balance of 2026

One free-to-attend and one paid/ticketed opportunity every week. Rows marked CONFIRM are real, recurring channels that need a specific date booked before the week arrives — treat those as this week's to-do, not a gap in the plan.

How the calendar buttons work

Each "Add to Calendar" button opens Google Calendar's event-creation screen pre-filled with the event details, so whoever clicks it can save it straight to their own Gmail calendar in one step. This does not require any special login or integration — it works the same way a "share to calendar" link on any event page does. It adds the event to the calendar of whoever is signed into Google in that browser, so each rep should click their own assigned events.

Confirmed items are sourced from Dallas College, Workforce Solutions Greater Dallas, the Dallas Regional Chamber, and national association calendars as of July 2026. Marketing Collateral Checklist

Built for the qualify-only model: nothing here can be mistaken for an enrollment offer, a rate quote, or a contract.

Table & signage

  • Tabletop banner: NCCB Credit branding, Pedison navy/light-blue palette, OCCC license number displayed
  • Retractable step-and-repeat or pop-up banner for larger booths
  • "Private Consultation" tent card for the sound-buffered chair
  • QR code table tent linking to the qualifier intake form

Printed handouts

  • "What Is a Debt Management Plan?" one-pager (educational only, no rate/savings claims)
  • OCCC license & fee-schedule disclosure sheet (available on request, not pushed)
  • General financial-literacy handout for non-qualifying prospects (no follow-up contact required)
  • Business cards for each field rep

Digital / on-site tools

  • Laptop with secure connection to the sales desk (phone/video line, office-hours extension list)
  • Tablet or laptop running the qualifier intake form (same pattern as the existing candidate-interview intake tool)
  • Charging bank/power strip, mobile hotspot as network backup
  • Printed office-hours & extension card taped inside the laptop lid

Scripts & training aids

  • Qualifier script card (approach → 3–5 screening questions → consent line)
  • "Do not say" card: no rate quotes, no savings promises, no program guarantees
  • Live-transfer phone script (what the rep says handing the prospect to the desk)
  • Objection-handling card for common prospect questions at the table

Compliance essentials (non-negotiable)

  • OCCC license number visible at every table, every event, no exceptions
  • Written contact-consent capture before any callback is scheduled (TCPA)
  • No cash, card, or bank information ever collected in the field
  • No enrollment agreement signed outside the licensed sales desk

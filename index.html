Preliminary Title Findings

Both title commitments returned recorded liens and open matters that we will need documentation and clarification on before underwriting can finalize a structure. This is routine for a facility of this size. We just need the full picture in writing from your side.

No.ItemWhat we need from you
1 Recorded liens on both properties, four each, totaling roughly $16.0M combined face value across both entities per the two title commitments Current payoff statements for each lienholder: Prudential/CMBS trust, Invigo Capital LLC, Yaron and Yael Levy Revocable Trust, and GPMC Hospitalities LLC, so we can confirm actual net equity position ahead of any new facility
2 Yaron and Yael Levy Revocable Trust deed of trust: a notice of trustee's sale has been recorded on both properties Written confirmation directly from the substitute trustee that the loan has been reinstated and no sale has occurred or is scheduled. Title requires this in writing. A payoff statement alone will not satisfy the requirement.
3 Pending lawsuit: Yaron and Yael Levy versus Alyna West, Alyna East, and Rakesh Patel individually, 73rd District Court, Bexar County Status update, and if resolved or in settlement, supporting documentation or a release to be filed
4 Pending lawsuit: Regions Bank versus Alyna East, Alyna West, Rakesh Patel, and Reena Patel, 455th District Court, Travis County Status update and current standing of this matter
5 Condemnation proceeding noted against Alyna East, State of Texas, Probate Court No. 1, Travis County Background on the scope of the action and expected impact, if any, on the East property or its valuation
6 Entity documentation required under Title Schedule C for both LLCs Thank you for the West Company Agreement. That's received. Still outstanding: a current certificate of good standing for each entity (the 2006 formation certificates on file are not sufficient for Schedule C purposes).
7 Property performance data We have STR reports for the Super 8/East property for most of 2025. April and August were not in the set provided. We would appreciate the complete run, plus equivalent STR or comparable performance data for the West/Quality Inn property.
8 Membership interest and collateral authority. Both Company Agreements on file show Rakesh Patel and Reena Patel each holding a 50% membership interest in Alyna East and Alyna West, respectively Please confirm Reena Patel's current membership interest in each entity and her present marital status relative to you. In Texas, spousal or co-owner joinder is often required on any document that pledges or encumbers real property, so we'll need this confirmed early to determine what additional signatures or consents will be required for the collateral pledge and any resulting loan documents.

None of the above is unusual for a transaction at this size. Every institutional lender will ask for the same package. Once we have the payoff statements, the trustee confirmation on the Levy loan, and status on the two open matters, we will be in a much better position to size a structure that works for both sides.
